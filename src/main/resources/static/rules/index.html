How to play

A step by step view for players who have never played Snake before.

Start a run

  • Launch the desktop client.
  • Choose a map, mode, and difficulty (if prompted).
  • Press Start to spawn the snake.
  • Food appears randomly on the map.

Move and turn

  • Use arrow keys or WASD to change direction.
  • You can only turn 90 degrees.
  • Most modes prevent reversing into yourself.
  • Stay inside the play area unless a map allows wrapping.

Core rules

These rules apply across all maps and modes unless noted.

Movement rules

  • The snake moves one grid unit per tick.
  • Turning is instant at the next grid step.
  • Speed increases at defined score thresholds.
  • Pausing a run freezes the timer and movement.

Collision rules

  • Hitting a wall ends the run.
  • Running into your own body ends the run.
  • Obstacle collisions end the run on blocked maps.
  • Border wrapping (if enabled) replaces wall collision.

Eating food grows the snake and increases score. Optional items can expand the challenge.

Optional items add extra depth.

Modes change objectives and how maps are selected.

Difficulty tiers

Difficulty adjusts speed, spawn pacing, and scoring.

Available difficulties

  • EASY: slower speed, generous turn window.
  • NORMAL: baseline speed and scoring.
  • HARD: faster ticks, tighter recovery.
  • EXPERT: high speed with minimal forgiveness.
  • INSANE: fastest pace, high risk.

What changes

  • Movement speed and acceleration thresholds.
  • Spawn rate for food or bonus items.
  • Score multipliers or end of run bonuses.
  • Leaderboard filters reflect difficulty.

Scoring and progress

Scoring combines base points with bonuses.

Score basics

  • Food pickup adds a base score.
  • Speed tiers can add a multiplier.
  • Difficulty can add a flat bonus.
  • Optional bonus items add extra points.
score = (food * 10) + (time * 1) + (difficultyBonus)

Progress stages

  • Early game: focus on safe turns and space.
  • Mid game: speed increases, routes tighten.
  • Late game: avoid self traps and plan exits.
  • End: run ends on collision or objective completion.

Game features

Systems that support gameplay and leaderboards.

LB

Online leaderboard

Runs can submit scores to the backend API.

Scores filter by map, mode, difficulty.

SS

Session protected submits

Submissions require a short lived session.

Helps reduce spam and replays.

OF

Offline play

You can still play without a connection.

Leaderboard submits may fail while offline.

Tips for new players

Simple advice to last longer and score higher.

Movement tips

  • Keep to the edges early to avoid self traps.
  • Leave escape lanes when turning into tight spaces.
  • Plan two moves ahead at higher speeds.

Scoring tips

  • Focus on survival over risky shortcuts.
  • Use easier maps to learn speed changes.
  • Practice a single map to master routes.
